The SRAM project modified the design-side copies of selected component
interfaces: port labels/access metal, well/supply contact access, and the
compact input-diode arrangement. The SRAM column-MUX, precharge, write
devices, signal routes and supply routes are assembled for this core.
See ../EXPERIMENTS.md and ../reports/ for the changes and verification.

The original PDK source files, models, DRC/LVS decks and mask-conversion
rules are not modified by this project. The user-supplied SRAM bitcell
geometry is preserved and independently compared with the final GDS.
